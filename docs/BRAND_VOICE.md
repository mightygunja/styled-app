# Styled Brand Voice

**Styled should feel like:**

> "A calm, confident presence that respects your decision."

---

## What This Means

### Calm
- No urgency tactics
- No countdown timers
- No "last chance" pressure
- No anxiety-inducing language
- Steady, reassuring tone

### Confident
- Clear about value
- No desperate upsells
- No over-explaining
- Trust in the product
- Professional, not salesy

### Respects Your Decision
- No guilt trips
- No dark patterns
- Easy cancellation
- Graceful exits
- "We trust you to decide"

---

## How This Shows Up

### In Trial Reminders
**Calm, confident presence:**
```
Your style is coming together.

You're still in your free trial. If Styled's been helpful, keep exploring — no action needed yet.
```

**Not:**
```
❌ Only 2 days left!
❌ Don't lose access!
❌ Act now or lose everything!
```

### In Upgrade Nudges
**Respects your decision:**
```
Based on your style preferences, Styled can keep refining this.

Try free for 7 days
```

**Not:**
```
❌ Unlock unlimited outfits NOW!
❌ Limited time offer!
❌ Don't miss out!
```

### In Post-Conversion
**Calm confidence:**
```
You're all set. Styled will keep working in the background for you.
```

**Not:**
```
❌ 🎉 Congratulations on upgrading!
❌ Welcome to our premium community!
❌ You made the right choice!
```

### In Cancellation
**Respects your decision:**
```
Thanks for the feedback.
Styled will keep improving — and you're always welcome back.
```

**Not:**
```
❌ Are you sure you want to cancel?
❌ Here's 50% off to stay!
❌ You'll lose all your progress!
```

---

## Copy Principles

### Always:
✅ State facts clearly
✅ Acknowledge their perspective
✅ Provide clear next steps
✅ Leave door open
✅ Professional tone

### Never:
❌ Create urgency
❌ Guilt trip
❌ Manipulate
❌ Oversell
❌ Pressure

---

## The Test

**For every piece of copy, ask:**

"Does this feel like a calm, confident presence that respects your decision?"

If not, rewrite it.

---

## Examples Across All Touchpoints

### Onboarding
**Calm, confident:**
```
Let's learn your style preferences.
This helps Styled suggest outfits that actually work for you.
```

### Sparse Closet
**Respects reality:**
```
You're starting with a focused wardrobe.
Styled works best when you add what you actually wear.
```

### Paywall
**Confident, not pushy:**
```
Styled is learning your preferences.
Keeping it active helps your wardrobe work together over time.
```

### Trial Status Card
**Calm information:**
```
Free trial active
Ends in 2 days

You won't be charged until the trial ends.
```

### Day-14 Retention
**Reinforces value:**
```
Your wardrobe is starting to work together.

Styled has been learning your preferences and refining how pieces work together. This gets better the longer you use it.
```

### Subscription Management
**Clear, transparent:**
```
You're subscribed to Styled Plus

$8.99/month
Renews on Feb 14

[Manage in App Store]
```

### App Store Review Reply
**Professional, empathetic:**
```
Thanks for trying Styled — and for flagging this.

We do our best to clearly show when trials end and that subscriptions are managed through the App Store, but we understand it can still feel surprising.
```

## Voice Characteristics

### Tone
- Professional, not corporate
- Friendly, not overly casual
- Helpful, not pushy
- Honest, not salesy

### Language
- Clear, simple words
- Short sentences
- Active voice
- No jargon

### Personality
- Trustworthy
- Competent
- Respectful
- Calm

---

## What Styled Is NOT

❌ **Not aggressive** - No hard sells, no pressure
❌ **Not desperate** - No discounts to prevent churn
❌ **Not manipulative** - No dark patterns, no tricks
❌ **Not anxious** - No urgency, no countdown timers
❌ **Not salesy** - No hype, no over-promising

---

## What Styled IS

✅ **Calm** - Steady, reassuring presence
✅ **Confident** - Trust in the product's value
✅ **Respectful** - Honors user agency and choice
✅ **Professional** - Competent, reliable, trustworthy
✅ **Helpful** - Genuinely useful, not just selling

---

## Remember

**Every interaction should reinforce:**

"Styled is a calm, confident presence that respects your decision."

This isn't just marketing copy. It's the foundation of how Styled treats users at every touchpoint.

**When you respect users, they respect you back.**
