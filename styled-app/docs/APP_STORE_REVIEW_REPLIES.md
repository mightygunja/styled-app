# App Store Review Replies for Trial Complaints

**Use these verbatim or lightly customize. Apple loves this tone.**

---

## A. "I was charged without realizing" (Most Common)

### Reply (Exact)

```
Thanks for trying Styled — and for flagging this.

We do our best to clearly show when trials end and that subscriptions are managed through the App Store, but we understand it can still feel surprising.

You can always manage or cancel your subscription directly in your App Store settings. If you have trouble finding it, our support team is happy to help.

We appreciate you giving Styled a try.
```

### Why This Works

✅ **Takes responsibility without admitting wrongdoing**
- "We do our best" = acknowledges effort
- "Can still feel surprising" = validates their feeling
- No "you should have known"

✅ **Reinforces transparency**
- Points to App Store settings
- Offers support help
- No deflection

✅ **Defuses emotion**
- Calm, professional tone
- No defensiveness
- Appreciates their feedback

---

## A2. "I forgot to cancel" (Very Common - Alternative)

### Reply (Exact)

```
Thanks for giving Styled a try.

Trials automatically convert unless canceled through Apple, which we try to communicate clearly in-app. That said, we understand life gets busy.

You can manage your subscription anytime in App Store settings, and we appreciate you taking the time to share your experience.
```

### Why This Works

✅ **Validates without blame**
- "Life gets busy" = empathy
- No "you should have remembered"

✅ **States facts calmly**
- Explains auto-conversion
- References in-app communication
- No defensiveness

✅ **Provides solution**
- Clear next steps
- Appreciates feedback

---

## B. "Trial ended too quickly"

### Reply

```
Thanks for the feedback.

Our 7-day trial is designed to give you time to explore Styled and see if it fits your routine. We understand that might not be enough time for everyone.

If you'd like to continue exploring, you can manage your subscription in the App Store settings, or reach out to our support team if you have questions.

We appreciate you trying Styled.
```

### Why This Works

- Explains trial length rationale
- Validates their concern
- Offers clear next steps
- No pressure to continue

---

## C. "Couldn't figure out how to cancel"

### Reply

```
We're sorry you had trouble canceling.

All subscriptions are managed through the App Store (not within the Styled app itself). You can cancel anytime by going to Settings → [Your Name] → Subscriptions on your iPhone.

If you need help with this, our support team is happy to walk you through it.

Thanks for trying Styled, and we hope the cancellation process is clearer now.
```

### Why This Works

- Apologizes for confusion
- Provides clear instructions
- Offers direct help
- Educational, not defensive

---

## D. "Too expensive" (Never Justify Pricing)

### Reply (Exact)

```
Thanks for sharing your thoughts.

We aim to price Styled in a way that supports thoughtful design and ongoing improvements, but we know value looks different for everyone.

We appreciate you taking the time to try it and leave feedback.
```

### Why This Works

✅ **No justification**
- Doesn't defend the price
- Doesn't explain value
- Just acknowledges feedback

✅ **Explains intent without selling**
- "Supports thoughtful design" = honest
- Not "here's why it's worth it"

✅ **Respects perspective**
- "Value looks different for everyone" = true
- No attempt to convince
- Graceful acceptance

## G. "Felt like a scam" (Rare but serious)

### Reply

```

Styled is a small team working to build a helpful, transparent app. We show trial terms upfront, and all subscriptions are managed through Apple's system (not ours).

If there's something specific we could have done better to be clearer, we'd genuinely appreciate hearing about it at [support email].

Thanks for giving us a chance, and we're sorry we didn't meet your expectations.
```

### Why This Works

- Takes it seriously
- Humanizes the team
- Reinforces transparency
- Genuine apology
- Invites feedback

## General Principles

### Always:
✅ Thank them for trying
✅ Validate their feeling
✅ Provide clear next steps
✅ Offer support help
✅ Stay professional and calm

### Never:
❌ Get defensive
❌ Blame the user
❌ Make excuses
❌ Argue about facts
❌ Ignore negative reviews

---

## Response Template

**For any complaint:**

```
[Thank them for feedback]

[Validate their concern without admitting fault]

[Provide helpful information or next steps]

[Offer support if needed]

[Close with appreciation]
```

---

## Timing

**Respond within:**
- 24 hours for negative reviews
- 48 hours for neutral reviews
- 1 week for positive reviews

**Why:**
- Shows you care
- Demonstrates responsiveness
- Improves App Store perception
- Can turn detractors into advocates

---

## What Apple Looks For

Apple favors developers who:
- ✅ Respond to reviews professionally
- ✅ Take responsibility
- ✅ Offer solutions
- ✅ Show commitment to improvement
- ✅ Maintain calm, helpful tone

This can influence:
- App Store featuring
- Search ranking
- Review moderation
- Overall relationship with Apple

---

## Success Metrics

### Good Signs
- ✅ Users update reviews after response
- ✅ "Developer is responsive" comments
- ✅ Improved rating over time
- ✅ Fewer repeat complaints

### Warning Signs
- ⚠️ No response to reviews
- ⚠️ Defensive responses
- ⚠️ Same complaints recurring
- ⚠️ Users escalate to Apple

---

## Remember

**Every review response is a public demonstration of your values.**

Users reading reviews see:
- How you handle criticism
- How you treat customers
- Whether you take responsibility
- If you're worth trusting

**Respond like the whole world is watching. Because they are.**
