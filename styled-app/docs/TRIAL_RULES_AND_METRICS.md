# Trial Rules and Health Metrics

**Critical: Apple enforces one free trial per subscription group. Plan accordingly.**

---

## Critical Trial Principles

### Uniform Trial Length (REQUIRED)

**Do NOT do different trial lengths per tier.**

❌ **Bad:**
- Plus: 7-day trial
- Premium: 14-day trial

✅ **Good:**
- Plus: 7-day trial
- Premium: 7-day trial

**Why uniform trials matter:**
- Creates clarity (no confusion)
- Builds confidence (fair treatment)
- Prevents comparison anxiety ("Why does Premium get more time?")
- Simplifies messaging
- Shows respect for all users

**This is founder-level advice. Don't overthink it.**

---

## One-Trial Rule (Do NOT Stack)

### Apple's Rule

**One free trial per subscription group, ever.**

Even if they:
- Cancel and resubscribe
- Switch from Plus to Premium
- Downgrade from Premium to Plus
- Delete and reinstall app

**They only get ONE trial across the entire subscription group.**

### Why This Is Good

✅ **Prevents gaming:**
- Users can't cancel and restart trials
- No trial hopping between tiers
- Clean, honest system

✅ **Keeps trust intact:**
- No confusing "why can't I trial again?"
- Clear expectations
- Professional approach

✅ **Forces good onboarding:**
- Must show value in first trial
- Can't rely on multiple trial attempts
- Better product discipline

```

├── Plus Monthly ($8.99)

```

```
Day 1: Start Premium trial

✅ Works, but no new trial
```

## Trial Health Metrics

### Target Ranges

| Metric | Healthy Range | Warning | Critical |
|--------|--------------|---------|----------|
| **Trial → Paid conversion** | 40–60% | 30–40% | <30% |
| **Trial cancellation before Day 7** | <30% | 30–40% | >40% |
| **Trial churn reviews** | ~0 | 1–2 | >2 |

---

## Trial → Paid Conversion: 40–60%

### What This Means
- 4-6 out of 10 trial users convert to paid
- Industry benchmark: 30-50%
- Target is strong conversion

### How to Hit It
✅ **Show value during trial:**
- Generate 5+ outfits in trial period
- Show "Why this works" explanations
- Mark outfits as worn (validation)
- Display usage stats

✅ **Remind of value:**
- Day 3: "You've created 5 outfits this week"
- Day 5: "Your trial ends in 2 days"
- Day 6: Show what they'll lose

✅ **Make cancellation easy:**
- No dark patterns
- Clear cancellation flow
- Builds trust

### If <40% Conversion

**Diagnose:**
- Are users generating outfits during trial?
- Are they marking outfits as worn?
- Are they returning daily?
- Do they understand the value?

**Fix:**
- Improve onboarding
- Show value faster
- Better trial reminders
- Clearer value proposition

---

## Trial Cancellation Before Day 7: <30%

### What This Means
- Less than 3 out of 10 users cancel before trial ends
- Healthy: Users are engaged
- Unhealthy: Users bail early

### How to Hit It
✅ **Deliver value immediately:**
- First outfit within 5 minutes
- "Why this works" on first outfit
- Instant confidence boost

✅ **Build habit quickly:**
- Day 1: Generate first outfit
- Day 2: Morning notification
- Day 3: Generate second outfit
- Day 4: Show usage stats

✅ **No surprises:**
- Clear trial terms
- Visible trial countdown
- Easy cancellation

### If >30% Cancellation

**Diagnose by timing:**

**Day 1 cancellations (>10%):**
- 🚨 Paywall too early
- 🚨 Value not clear
- 🚨 Onboarding too complex

**Day 2-3 cancellations (>15%):**
- ⚠️ Not enough value delivered
- ⚠️ Outfit quality issues
- ⚠️ Habit not forming

**Day 4-6 cancellations (<10%):**
- ✅ Normal (users testing)
- ✅ Some churn is healthy

---

## Trial Churn Reviews: ~0

### What This Means
- Users who cancel trial don't leave negative reviews
- Indicates respectful, fair trial experience
- Critical for App Store rating

### How to Hit It
✅ **Fair trial experience:**
- No dark patterns
- Easy cancellation
- Clear terms
- No surprise charges

✅ **Value before trial:**
- 3 free outfits before trial
- Show value first
- Trial is optional upgrade

✅ **Graceful exit:**
- "Thanks for trying Styled"
- Keep free features available
- No guilt trips

### If >0 Churn Reviews

**Red flags:**
- "Charged without warning"
- "Couldn't cancel"
- "Trial was a scam"
- "Hidden fees"

**Fix immediately:**
- Review trial disclosure
- Simplify cancellation
- Add trial reminders
- Check billing logic

## Tier Switching Strategy

### Plus → Premium Upgrade

**No trial needed:**
- User already paying
- Upgrade is immediate
- Pro-rate billing

**Messaging:**
```
"Upgrade to Premium for $23.99/month
Get personal stylist review and priority support"
```

### Premium → Plus Downgrade

**No trial needed:**
- Downgrade at end of billing period
- Keep Premium until then
- No refunds

**Messaging:**
```
"Downgrade to Plus at end of billing period
You'll keep Premium features until [date]"
```

### Trial → Cancel → Resubscribe

**No trial available:**
- User already used trial
- Must subscribe directly
- Full price from Day 1

**Messaging:**
```
"Welcome back to Styled!
Subscribe to Plus for $8.99/month
(Trial not available - already used)"
```

## Red Flags

### Critical Issues
- 🚨 Trial conversion <30%
- 🚨 Day 1 cancellations >15%
- 🚨 Negative reviews about trials
- 🚨 Billing complaints

### Warning Signs
- ⚠️ Conversion declining week-over-week
- ⚠️ High Day 2-3 cancellations
- ⚠️ Users not engaging during trial
- ⚠️ Low trial start rate

---

## Success Criteria

**Trial system is healthy when:**
- ✅ Conversion rate 40-60%
- ✅ Early cancellations <30%
- ✅ Zero churn reviews
- ✅ Users engage during trial
- ✅ Clear, fair trial experience
